# Frontend assets

- Host Grotesk: Google Fonts, bundled locally in `fonts/host-grotesk.ttf`. See
  `fonts/host-grotesk-license.txt` for the SIL Open Font License and copyright.
- Instrument Sans: Google Fonts, bundled locally in `fonts/instrument-sans.ttf`.
  See `fonts/instrument-sans-license.txt` for the SIL Open Font License and copyright.
- Login vehicle photograph: Unsplash photo `photo-1503376780353-7e6692767b70`,
  bundled as `images/claims-vehicle.jpg`. Source:
  <https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1503376780353-7e6692767b70>.
  Unsplash license: <https://unsplash.com/license>.

Fonts and the login photograph are served by the application. No external font
or image service is required at runtime. Claim evidence and detector images
continue to come from the authenticated evidence APIs.
